A fix for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2816239831]Mega Trading (Additional Trade Routes)[/url] by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/id/UnicronNinja]Unicorn Ninja[/url], which no longer works with the current game.

ORIGINAL DESCRIPTION:

Removes the cap on Maximum trade routes. BNW & G+K* required,

- Harbours now provide 3 additional trade routes
- Caravansaries now provide 1 additional trade routes
- Colossus and Petra now provide 10 additional trade routes each
- The East India Company wonder now provides 100 additional trade routes
- Upon researching globalization 94 additional trade routes are made available, making the base number of trade routes 100 if no buildings are constructed.

Please keep in mind there is a cap on trade routes for a reason. Changes made by this mod could result in severely unbalanced gameplay, use at own risk!

This is my first Mod which I created for personal use. I am not an active modder and any bug fixes/updates/suggestions will only be added when and if I feel like it.
*BNW is required. I have both expansions so am unsure if G+K is also needed.
