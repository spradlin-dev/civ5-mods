[h1]Claude Player[/h1]
Play Civilization V with Claude, Anthropic's AI, as a teammate or a rival. Claude takes over one of the AI civilizations and plays it itself: it researches, builds, fights, trades and talks to the other leaders. A "Claude Log" button shows what Claude is thinking each turn.

[h2]What you need[/h2]
[list]
[*]Civilization V with Brave New World, on Windows
[*]Claude Code, with a Claude Pro or Max subscription or an API key: https://claude.com/claude-code
[/list]

[h2]Setup (one time)[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to this mod.
[*]Install Claude Code.
[*]Open a terminal and run:
[code]claude mcp add --transport http --scope user civ5 http://127.0.0.1:7676/mcp[/code]
[*]Make a folder for Claude's Civ games, for example Documents\Civ5 Claude. Always start Claude from that folder, so it remembers what it learns between games.
[/olist]

[h2]Playing a game[/h2]
[olist]
[*]In Civilization V, open Mods, enable Claude Player, and start a single-player game. Use Advanced Setup if you want Claude on your team.
[*]Once the map has loaded, open a terminal in your Claude folder and run: claude
[*]Tell Claude which civilization to play, for example: "Play the Maya in my Civ 5 game" or have it pick one at random.
[*]Play your turns as usual. Claude plays its turn after yours. Keep Claude running; it waits for its turn by itself.
[/olist]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Claude can only connect while a game is loaded.
[*]The connection stays on your own PC. Nothing in the game is reachable from the internet.
[*]Saves keep Claude's seat. After loading a save, start Claude again and it picks up where it left off.
[*]While Claude takes its turn, you can't act or save until it ends its turn. Keyboard shortcuts you press while claude is taking its turn will take effect after claude ends its turn.
[*]This mod replaces the game's core DLL, so it can't be combined with other mods that do the same, such as the Community Patch.
[*]Single player only. Multiplayer and hotseat are not supported.
[/list]
