# Local MCP Server DLL changelog

Versions are the mod's version numbers as the game shows them. A number missing from the list was a build that was never published; its changes are in the next version listed.

## Version 47 (2026-10-20)

- The AI can now see what another civilization has to offer in a trade, and what it could offer them, the way the trade screen shows it to you: gold, gold per turn, spare luxury and strategic resources, cities, embassies, open borders, defensive pacts, research agreements, and wars or peace with other civilizations. It no longer has to guess and try one offer after another.

## Version 46 (2026-10-20)

- The AI now sees every civilization's teammates and its wars, friendships and denouncements with the civilizations it has met, as on the game's Global Relationships screen. It also sees when you, a human player, have declared friendship with it or denounced it.

## Version 45 (2026-10-20)

- The Maya long count follows the game's own rules again: the Maya can take a Great Prophet before founding a pantheon, and can take a great person a second time once every type has been taken once.
- When a great person choice is refused, the AI is told the real rule.

## Version 44 (2026-10-19)

- When the AI picks a free great person (from faith, a policy or the Maya long count), it is only offered the great people its civilization can have, as the game's own window does. Another civilization's unique great person, such as the Merchant of Venice, is no longer offered.

## Version 43 (2026-10-19)

- An AI that takes a computer player's seat before anyone has moved now starts with a human player's units. The extra starting units computer players get on harder difficulties are removed.

## Version 42 (2026-10-19)

- When the AI asks a computer leader for a Declaration of Friendship, it hears the leader's answer in words (too soon, asked recently, yes or no), the same text you would see, in the game's language.

## Version 41 (2026-10-19)

- The AI can no longer put a Declaration of Friendship in a trade deal with a computer player, since the game only allows that between human players. It asks for friendship in conversation instead, with the same waiting rules you have.

## Version 40 (2026-10-18)

- The AI can look at a picture of the map around a point, in the style of the strategic view.
- Pressing Enter in the log window's chat box no longer also ends your turn.

## Version 39 (2026-10-18)

- The test link that eliminated your civilization is gone from the log window.

## Version 38 (2026-10-18)

- Fixed the screen freezing during an AI's turn in some situations.

## Version 37 (2026-10-18)

- A Go to link in the log window glides the camera to the tile again.

## Version 36 (2026-10-18)

- While you watch on after being eliminated, the game treats the AI civilizations as computer players, so the turns keep flowing.

## Version 35 (2026-10-18)

- A Go to link in the log window jumps the camera to the tile at once.

## Version 34 (2026-10-18)

- Turns keep playing live on screen while you watch on after being eliminated.

## Version 33 (2026-10-18)

- After your civilization is eliminated you can watch the rest of the game from an observer slot.

## Version 32 (2026-10-18)

- Added a test link in the log window that eliminates your civilization, for trying out watching on. Removed again in version 39.

## Version 31 (2026-10-18)

- The resource icon tooltip fix is now its own mod.

## Version 30 (2026-10-18)

- The resource icon tooltip is padded instead of moved, so it stays clear of the busy cursor.
- Shorter note about AI tokens in the Workshop description.

## Version 28 (2026-10-18)

- The resource icon tooltip no longer hides under the busy cursor.

## Version 27 (2026-10-18)

- The tile tooltip no longer hides under the busy cursor.

## Version 26 (2026-10-18)

- The tile info box above the minimap shows the tile's coordinates, so you can find the tiles the AI names.

## Version 25 (2026-10-18)

- A city you had seen stays on your map after another civilization captures it out of your sight.

## Version 24 (2026-10-18)

- Your AI teammate's turn now plays out live on screen.
- The AI is told whose unit is blocking a tile, can see the buildings in a city, and is told why a move was dropped.

## Version 13 (2026-10-18)

- After a deal is accepted, the AI is told which of its other offers that deal made pointless.

## Version 12 (2026-10-18)

- The chat uses the game's own chime.
- The AI drops offers that are out of date, and is told when the game cancels one of its moves.

## Version 10 (2026-10-18)

- The AI is told exactly why an attack or a move was refused.

## Version 9 (2026-10-17)

- Chat lines in the log window are colored, a chime plays when the AI replies, and the AI's turn banner is shown.

## Version 5 (2026-10-17)

- You can chat with an AI teammate from the log window.

## Version 4 (2026-10-17)

- Log lines that name a tile have a Go to link that moves the camera there.

## Version 3 (2026-10-15)

- The Workshop description says a long game needs plenty of AI tokens.

## Version 2 (2026-10-14)

- Fixed the log window not loading.

## Version 1 (2026-10-14)

- The AI can hand scouts and workers to the game's automation.
- First numbered version.

## Before version 1 (2026-10-10 to 2026-10-14)

Development builds that were never published. They built the mod: an AI playing a civilization, the log window, seats for several AIs, diplomacy and trade, and watching on after elimination.
